DS-GEN-00 Rev A: General Casualty Actions

1. All casualties
1.1 Report the casualty, the space and any injuries to Damage Control Central and sound the alarm.
1.2 Confirm the exact compartment before sending a response team.
1.3 Account for all personnel in and near the affected space.

2. Fire
2.1 Send the duty fire team with the nearest approved extinguishing agent.
2.2 Secure ventilation and non-essential power to the affected space.
2.3 Start the primary fire pump for the affected zone.
2.4 If the primary fire pump is unavailable, start the standby fire pump.
2.5 If the standby fire pump is unavailable, start the emergency fire pump.
2.6 Set fire boundaries around the affected space.

3. Smoke
3.1 Treat smoke as a fire until the source is found.
3.2 Require breathing protection for anyone entering smoke.

4. Flooding
4.1 Find the source of the flooding and report the rate of rise.
4.2 Secure electrical power that could contact the water.
4.3 Start dewatering with the nearest approved pump.
4.4 Set flooding boundaries around the affected space.

5. Electrical
5.1 De-energize the affected circuit before anyone touches it or applies water.
5.2 Keep personnel clear until the circuit is confirmed dead.

6. Fuel or oil spill
6.1 Stop the leak at the nearest isolation valve.
6.2 Remove ignition sources from the area.
6.3 Contain the spill and keep personnel clear.

7. Steam
7.1 Isolate the steam source and clear personnel from the space.

8. Toxic gas
8.1 Evacuate the space and require breathing protection before re-entry.
8.2 Test the atmosphere before declaring the space safe.

9. Injury
9.1 Send the medical team to the casualty.
9.2 Move an injured person only if the space is unsafe.

10. Loss of power
10.1 Report the loss of power to the Bridge and shift to the alternate source.

11. Loss of steering
11.1 Shift to the alternate steering station and warn nearby traffic.

12. Magazine
12.1 Report the magazine temperature and prepare the sprinkling system.
