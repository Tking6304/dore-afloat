DS-B-CCP-07 Rev C: Diesel Space Fire

4. Immediate actions
4.1 Report the fire to the Damage Control Central watch and sound the alarm.
4.2 Trip the diesel engine and close the fuel quick-closing valves for the affected space.
4.3 Secure supply and exhaust fans to the affected space.
4.4 Start Fire Pump 1 and pressurize the forward firemain loop.
4.5 If Fire Pump 1 is unavailable, start the emergency diesel fire pump.
4.5.1 If the emergency diesel fire pump is unavailable, rig the portable fire pump.
4.6 Set fire boundaries around the affected space.
