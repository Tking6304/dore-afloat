DS-CCP-COL-02 Rev A: Collision and Flooding

2. Immediate actions
2.1 Sound the collision alarm and pass the word on the general announcing system.
2.2 Set maximum watertight integrity throughout the ship.
2.3 Report damage, flooding and injuries to Damage Control Central.
2.4 Start Main Drainage Eductor 2 to dewater the affected space.
2.5 If Main Drainage Eductor 2 is unavailable, rig the portable dewatering pump.
2.5.1 If the portable dewatering pump is unavailable, start Eductor 1 and align it through the cross-connect.
2.6 Set flooding boundaries around the affected space.
2.7 Muster all hands and report missing personnel to the bridge.
