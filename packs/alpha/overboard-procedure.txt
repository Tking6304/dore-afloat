DS-CCP-MOB-03 Rev A: Man Overboard

2. Immediate actions
2.1 Pass the word man overboard and state the side.
2.2 Post lookouts and keep the person in sight.
2.3 Mark the position on the navigation display and log the time.
2.4 Turn the ship toward the side the person fell from to keep the stern clear.
2.5 Launch the rescue boat using Davit 1.
2.6 If Davit 1 is unavailable, rig the recovery net and stand by the rescue swimmer.
2.6.1 If the recovery net is unavailable, rig the Jacob's ladder and stand by the rescue swimmer.
2.7 Muster all hands and report missing personnel to the bridge.
