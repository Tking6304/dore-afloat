DS-SOP-TAG-01 Rev A: Tagged Equipment

2. Rules
2.1 Equipment under an active tag-out shall not be operated until the authorizing officer clears the tag-out.
2.2 During a casualty, use the next approved option and report the tagged equipment to the Engineering Officer of the Watch.
